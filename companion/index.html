What it does

Everything the meter can tell you — read on your phone, filed to your account.

The app doesn't just relay raw numbers off the sensor. Every colorimetric value it shows is computed with the exact same math hCRI.io itself uses, so your phone and your account never disagree about what a scan means.

Live spectral power distribution

The full wavelength-by-wavelength curve, straight off the sensor, charted the moment a reading finishes — the same view you'd get plugged into a PC.

CIE 1931 chromaticity plot

x, y, CCT and Duv plotted against the real spectral locus — derived from the reading's spectrum, not the meter's own onboard registers, which can drift between firmware states.

Ra, R9, and the full R1–R15 set

Every CRI sample computed with hCRI.io's own ported CIE 13.3 algorithm — the same Ra/R9 your report page will show once the scan is uploaded — plotted as the same color-coded R1–R15 bar chart hCRI.io's own report page uses, right on the Spectrum tab.

Pick your own measurements

Choose which values show on the Main tab's result card, and in what order, from Settings — CCT, Ra, Rf, Rg, any of R1–R15, even CIE x/y, drag-reordered by holding the handle on each row. The rest stay a tap away without cluttering the card after every reading.

One-tap upload, from wherever you are

Connect your account once — generate a token right in the app with your hCRI.io username and password, or paste in one you've already made. From then on, a reading is one button away from becoming a report — the Upload button lives on both the Main tab and the Data tab, so you're never more than a tap from sending a scan.

Upload preview

See the exact wavelength/value pairs about to be sent before you send them — the same payload hCRI.io will recompute everything from.

Every reading, saved automatically

The History tab keeps a running log of everything you've scanned. Rename a reading, re-upload it later, or delete it — renaming before upload sends it to hCRI.io under that same title. Tap View on any past reading to reopen its full measurement grid, spectrum, CIE plot, and R-values, exactly as they looked the moment it was taken.

Bulk upload, straight from History

Tap Select on the History tab to check off any number of past readings and upload them to hCRI.io in one go — no need to open and send each one individually.

Export any reading as CSV

Share a single reading or your entire history as CSV straight from the Data or History tab — handy for a quick spreadsheet check without ever opening hCRI.io.

Reconnects itself, recovers from crashes

The app clears out stale Bluetooth connections on its own after a reload — no more power cycling the meter. If something still goes wrong, a crash lands on a "Try Again" screen instead of kicking you back to your home screen.

Built for a session at the light bench

A short haptic buzz confirms a reading finished (a different pattern if it failed) without needing to look at the screen, and an optional setting keeps the screen awake for as long as the meter stays connected.

Compatibility

Built for the HPCS family

Each model's own BLE layout is handled on its own terms — the app reads the advertised device name and picks the right field map automatically.

Privacy

Your readings stay yours

hCRI Companion doesn't collect, store, or transmit anything about you or your readings on its own -- there's no analytics, no tracking, no third-party server in the loop at all.

Nothing leaves your phone by default

A reading lives only on your device -- in its local History -- until you tap Upload. There's no background sync, no account required just to take a measurement, and no copy kept anywhere else.

One destination: your own hCRI.io account

Uploading sends the spectrum straight to the hCRI.io account you sign in with -- never to us, never to any other server. Your hCRI.io username and API token are stored only in your device's secure keychain, not in this app's own backend (it doesn't have one).

No analytics, no telemetry

No usage tracking, no crash reporting service, no advertising SDKs. The only network calls this app ever makes are the one you trigger with the Upload button.

How it works

From meter to report, in one sitting

Set the account connection up once. After that, every scan is a straight line to hCRI.io.

Connect your hCRI.io account

Open the app's Settings and tap Generate Token to sign in with your hCRI.io username and password and create a token right there, or paste in a token you've already created from Profile → API on the website. This only needs doing once.

Pair with your meter

On the Main tab, connect to your HPCS meter over Bluetooth, or use Scan for Nearby Meters if it isn't found automatically. If a reload ever leaves it stuck, the app clears the stale connection on its own.

Take a reading

Tap Take Reading. The spectrum, CCT, Duv, Ra and R9 appear as soon as the meter finishes its exposure, with a short buzz to confirm it landed.

Check the Spectrum tab

Swipe between the SPD curve, the CIE 1931 plot, and the R1–R15 bar chart to confirm the reading looks right before it goes anywhere.

Review, title, and upload

On the Data tab, open Upload Preview to see exactly what will be sent, optionally give the scan a title, then tap Upload to hCRI.io -- or upload straight from the Main tab if you'd rather not switch.

Come back to it anytime

Every reading is kept in History. Rename it, re-upload it later, share it (or your whole history) as a CSV, or delete it -- whenever you get to it. Tap View on any of them to reopen its full measurement grid, spectrum, CIE plot, and R-values.

Find it in your account

The scan appears in your hCRI.io reports moments later, with Ra, R9, Rf, Rg and the full spectrum recomputed server-side.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

hCRI Companion ("the app") is an independent Bluetooth client for Hopoocolor HPCS-series spectrometers, built to read spectral data and upload it to your own hCRI.io account. The app has no backend server of its own, and does not collect, sell, or share your data with any third party. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service built into the app.

Data stored on your device

— Meter readings you take (spectral data, computed CCT/Ra/R9/etc., and any title you give a reading) are stored locally in the app's private storage until you delete them or uninstall the app.
— Your hCRI.io username and API token, if you sign in, are stored in your device's secure keychain (Android Keystore) — not in plain text, and not readable by other apps.
— The Bluetooth ID of the most recently connected meter is stored locally so the app can reconnect to it faster next time.

Data you choose to share

— Tapping "Upload to hCRI.io" sends that one reading's spectral data and title to your own hCRI.io account over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
— If you use the optional "Generate Token" button in Settings instead of pasting in an existing API token, your hCRI.io username/email and password are sent once, over the same encrypted connection, to sign in and create a new token. Your password is never stored by the app and never leaves that one request.
— "Share CSV" and "Share Debug Log" hand data to Android's own share sheet so you can send it wherever you choose (email, messaging, etc.) — the app itself does not see or record where that goes.
— These, plus pairing with your meter over Bluetooth, are the only network/radio activity the app ever initiates, and only when you trigger them.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

— Bluetooth (scan / connect): required to find and read data from your spectrometer over Bluetooth Low Energy.
— Internet: required only to upload a reading to hCRI.io when you tap Upload.
— Vibrate: a short buzz confirming a reading finished — purely local, no data involved.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored readings and credentials are removed automatically if you uninstall hCRI Companion. You can also delete individual readings from the app's History tab at any time (tap the trash icon on any reading), or clear your saved hCRI.io credentials from Settings — both take effect immediately, with nothing further to request.

A reading you've uploaded to your hCRI.io account is kept there until you remove it yourself: sign in at hcri.io, open your Reports, and tap the trash icon on any report to delete it — the same action as deleting a reading in the app, just on the server copy. Deleting your hCRI.io account removes every report stored under it.

Children's privacy

hCRI Companion is a measurement tool for lighting and photography enthusiasts and professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
