Privacy Policy

Version 2026-07-02 · Last updated 2 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data hCRI.io ("we", "us", "the service") collects, why, and the choices and rights you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this service is fizzgig, North Carolina, United States. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact fizzgig@hcri.io.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, the display name you choose, and a securely hashed (never plain-text) version of your password.
  • Content you create — the spectral measurement files you upload and the reports generated from them (metrics such as CCT, Duv, CRI, TM-30, the spectral data, labels, categories, and whether you mark a report public or private).
  • Limited technical data — standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) kept briefly for security and to keep the service running.
  • A login token stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary for the service and is not used for tracking.
  • Analytics data. We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data, to understand aggregate usage (such as pages viewed and device/browser type). It cannot identify you individually.

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics service, to understand how the site is used in aggregate. Umami does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not collect personal data, so no consent banner is required. We do not run advertising and we do not sell your data.

3. Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the service (creating your account, storing and showing your reports) — performance of our agreement with you.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse — our legitimate interests.
  • To send essential service emails (for example password resets) — performance of our agreement with you.
  • Analytics — our legitimate interest in understanding aggregate site usage, using a cookieless, privacy-friendly tool that does not collect personal data.
  • Account consent, which you give when you create an account and accept this policy and our Terms. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

4. Public reports

If you mark a report as public, its contents and metrics become visible to anyone using the Explore section. Public reports do not display your name or email. You can make a report private or delete it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers we rely on to operate, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Hosting: Spaceship.
  • Email delivery: Spacemail.
  • Analytics: Umami, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics service.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including the United States. Where that happens for people in the EU/UK, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses provided by those processors.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you delete them. When you delete your account, your personal data and private reports are removed; public reports are either deleted or anonymised (with the link to you removed) according to your choice at deletion. Brief server logs are kept for a short period and then discarded.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (this list reflects the GDPR, and similar rights exist elsewhere), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data ("erasure");
  • receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

You can exercise the main rights yourself from Account settings: Export my data downloads everything we hold about you as a JSON file, and Delete my account erases it. For anything else, email fizzgig@hcri.io and we will respond within one month.

9. Children

The service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If we make material changes we will update the version and date above and, where appropriate, ask you to agree again.
