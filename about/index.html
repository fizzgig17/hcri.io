About hCRI.io

A free tool for LED and lighting enthusiasts

hCRI.io turns a spectral measurement into a full color-rendering report — CCT, Duv, CRI (Ra, R1–R15), and TM-30's Rf/Rg — the same numbers you'd get from calibrated lab equipment, computed from whatever spectrometer you actually have. Upload a CSV or JSON export from your meter, and it does the rest.

It started as a way to scratch a personal itch: flashlight and LED tinkering means caring a lot about how a light source actually renders color, and the tools for turning a raw spectrum into a readable report were either expensive, clunky, or both. hCRI.io is the tool I wanted — free, fast, and built around the spectrometers hobbyists actually own.

Who it's for

Anyone measuring light and wanting to understand what the numbers mean — flashlight and LED enthusiasts, photographers and videographers checking a light's color quality, or anyone curious how a bulb, panel, or fixture actually renders color rather than just what its box claims.

Get hCRI Companion

Take readings straight from your meter

hCRI Companion is the Android app for hCRI.io — it connects to your Hopoocolor HPCS-series spectrometer over Bluetooth and uploads a reading to your account in one tap, no cable or manual CSV export required.
