What am I looking at?

The image shows the same photo twice: the left side is the reference (true colours), the right side is how the chosen light renders them. Drag the divider to wipe between them.

The controls describe the light:

  • Fidelity (Rf) — overall colour accuracy.
  • Gamut (Rg) — overall saturation. Above 100 oversaturates, below shrinks.
  • R9 / R10 / R11 / R12 — how well each saturated colour renders: red, yellow, green, blue. Lower one to dull and shift that part of the spectrum. R9 (deep red) is the one that usually suffers most.

This is a teaching sandbox using a representative model, not a measured report. Photos are only used in the browser and are never uploaded to any server.
